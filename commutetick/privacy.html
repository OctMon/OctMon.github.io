Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the CommuteTick developer handles information when you use CommuteTick. Contact: octmon@qq.com. The app is free, has no advertising, requires no app account and has no developer-operated tracking or analytics server.

1. Information used on your device

After you start a trip and grant location permission, the app processes precise coordinates, timestamps, location accuracy and speed. It stores your routes, checkpoint names and positions, trips, passing events, predictions, corrections and settings on your device. These records support your timelines, history, statistics and personal forecasts. Recording is manual; the app does not automatically track you all day.

2. Background location and permission choices

An active trip can keep recording in the background and while the screen is locked, with the iOS location indicator. Pausing or finishing stops continuous location updates. You can change location and Precise Location permissions in iOS Settings. Denied permission, reduced accuracy, poor reception, system restrictions or force quitting can cause missing or less accurate records. The app does not read another navigation app's route or arrival estimates.

3. Apple Maps and Apple services

MapKit and Core Location provide maps, searches, reference routes, estimates and location. Using these services can send requests, search terms and relevant coordinates to Apple and the providers Apple uses for its services, under Apple's privacy terms. We do not operate those services or receive your Apple Account credentials. We do not sell your location, use it for advertising or combine it with advertising identifiers.

4. Optional iCloud Drive two-way sync

Sync is off by default. If you enable it, routes, precise tracks, checkpoint times, corrections and synchronization revisions are written to your own iCloud Drive and merged across devices using that iCloud account. Changes and deletions synchronize when processing and transfers complete. A locally generated installation identifier orders synchronization revisions; it is not an advertising identifier. Conflicting edits may retain both versions for review.

Recording takes priority: the app defers synchronization work during a trip. Apple controls cloud availability and upload/download timing. iCloud storage limits and Apple's terms apply. We do not provide a developer cloud account or a dashboard for browsing your records. Cloud sync is not an independent historical backup: deletion can propagate to other devices.

Turning sync off stops subsequent app synchronization; it does not delete existing cloud files. Synced files and revisions may remain in iCloud Drive. To remove all cloud copies, disable sync on all devices and remove the CommuteTick sync folder through Files/iCloud Drive, including Recently Deleted where appropriate. Separately exported files must be deleted where you stored them.

5. Sharing, photos and exported files

You choose whether to generate, save or share an image or export a complete backup. Sharing includes full routes, endpoints and exact times by default; review the preview and optional hiding controls before sharing. The app does not automatically publish or send your images. Selected share destinations handle the content under their own terms.

Saving an image requests Add Photos permission only. The app does not request access to read your entire photo library. Complete backups contain precise location and time information. Backup and sync files are not protected by a separate app password or app-level end-to-end encryption; device and iCloud protections apply. Keep your device, account and exported files secure.

6. Retention and deletion

Raw tracks and other records remain until you remove them. In Settings, you can clear raw tracks, delete all records, export a backup or restore a validated backup. Trip details allow individual trip deletion. Restoration replaces local records and disables sync; enabling it again merges cloud records. Synchronization revisions, conflict copies and cloud files can require the additional cloud deletion steps above.

The app database is excluded from system device backups. Without iCloud sync or a manual export, deleting the app or losing the device may permanently lose your records. Uninstalling does not itself remove iCloud files, shared images or exported backups. Temporary sharing files are cleaned on a later app launch when safe for completed exports.

7. Support emails and your choices

If you email octmon@qq.com, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request and resolve the reported issue, and retained only as necessary for that support purpose and applicable obligations. Email is handled by the email providers involved. Do not send a full location backup unless you intend to disclose it.

You can access, correct, export and delete app records on your device. We cannot retrieve a local database or your private iCloud Drive records on your behalf. For support correspondence, privacy requests or rights available under applicable law, contact the email above.

8. Children and changes

CommuteTick is a personal travel record tool and is not directed specifically to children. It has no child advertising or developer account-registration flow. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support mailbox, contact us. We may update this policy as features change; the current version and effective date are available here and in the app. Material changes to permissions or optional synchronization are explained in the relevant app flow.

隐私政策

本政策说明途刻（CommuteTick）开发者在你使用应用时如何处理信息。联系邮箱：octmon@qq.com。应用免费、无广告、无需应用账号，也没有开发者自建的轨迹或统计分析服务器。

1. 在设备上处理的信息

在你手动开始行程并授予定位权限后，应用处理精确坐标、时间戳、定位精度和速度，并在设备上保存路线、节点名称和位置、行程、经过事件、预测、修正及设置。这些数据用于时间线、历史、统计和个人预测。应用不会全天候自动开始追踪。

2. 后台定位与权限选择

进行中的行程可在后台及锁屏时继续记录，并显示 iOS 定位指示。暂停或结束后停止持续定位。你可以在系统设置中修改定位和精确位置权限。拒绝权限、降低精度、信号不佳、系统限制或强制关闭应用可能造成记录缺失或不准确。应用不会读取其他导航软件的路线或预计到达时间。

3. 苹果地图及苹果服务

应用通过 MapKit 和 Core Location 提供地图、搜索、参考路线、粗估和定位。使用这些服务时，请求、搜索词及相关坐标可能发送给苹果及其服务所使用的提供方，由苹果相关隐私条款约束。开发者不运营这些服务，也不会获得你的 Apple 账户凭据。我们不会出售位置数据、将其用于广告或与广告标识符合并。

4. 可选的 iCloud 云盘双向同步

同步默认关闭。开启后，路线、精确轨迹、站点时刻、修正及同步版本信息会写入你自己的 iCloud 云盘，并在使用该 iCloud 账号的设备间合并。修改和删除在处理及传输完成后同步。本地生成的安装标识用于排列同步版本，并非广告标识。发生并发编辑时可能保留两个版本以供核对。

记录行程优先：记录期间推迟同步处理。云端可用性及上传下载时机由苹果控制，并受 iCloud 空间及苹果条款约束。我们不提供开发者云账号，也没有用于浏览你记录的管理后台。同步不是独立的历史备份，删除可能传播到其他设备。

关闭开关会停止后续应用同步，不会删除已有云文件。同步文件及版本可能继续保留。若需删除全部云端副本，请先在所有设备关闭同步，再通过「文件」或 iCloud 云盘删除 CommuteTick 的同步文件夹，并按需清理「最近删除」。另行导出的文件需在保存位置自行删除。

5. 分享、相册和导出文件

是否生成、保存、分享图片或导出完整备份，由你选择。分享默认包含完整路线、起终点及具体时刻，请在预览中检查并使用隐藏选项。应用不会自动公开或发送图片；你选择的分享目标依据其自身条款处理内容。

保存图片时仅申请添加照片权限，不申请读取整个相册。完整备份包含精确位置和时间。备份及同步文件不另设应用密码，也不提供应用自行实现的端到端加密，使用设备与 iCloud 的保护机制。请保护设备、账号和导出文件。

6. 保存和删除

原始轨迹及其他记录持续保留，直到你主动清理。设置页可清理原始轨迹、删除全部记录、导出备份及恢复经过校验的备份；行程详情可删除单次行程。恢复会替换本地记录并关闭同步，重新开启后会与云记录合并。同步版本、冲突副本及云文件可能还需要执行前述云端删除步骤。

应用数据库不进入系统设备备份。未开启 iCloud 同步且未手动导出时，卸载或丢失设备可能永久丢失记录。卸载本身不会删除 iCloud 文件、已分享图片和导出备份。临时分享文件会在之后启动应用、适合清理已完成导出的时机清理。

7. 支持邮件和你的选择

当你发邮件至 octmon@qq.com 时，我们会收到你的地址、邮件内容及你主动提供的附件，仅用于回复请求、处理问题，并在支持所需及适用义务要求的时间内保留。邮件由相关邮件服务商处理。除非你愿意披露完整位置历史，请不要发送完整备份。

你可以在设备上查看、修正、导出和删除记录。开发者无法代为取回你的本地数据库或私人 iCloud 云盘记录。如需处理支持邮件、提出隐私请求或行使适用法律规定的权利，请使用上述邮箱联系。

8. 未成年人和政策更新

途刻是个人出行记录工具，并非专门面向儿童的产品，没有针对儿童的广告或开发者账号注册流程。如你认为未成年人向支持邮箱发送了个人信息，请联系我们。功能变化时可能更新本政策，当前版本和生效日期可在本页面及应用中查阅；权限及可选同步的重要变更会在相关流程中说明。
