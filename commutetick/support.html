Common questions

How do I record a trip?

On Today, choose Free recording or a route, then Start recording. Allow location access while using the app and enable Precise Location. You can view your position and track on the live map, or continue recording in the background with the iOS location indicator. Pause, continue or finish from the recording page or full-screen map.

Does it provide navigation or identify transit rides?

No. Use your preferred navigation app alongside it. Bus and metro stations are driving checkpoints; their passing times do not imply boarding or entering a station.

Why is a time missing or estimated?

Poor GPS reception, tunnels, reduced accuracy, pausing, permissions or force quitting can cause gaps. The app retains the distinction between actual observations, estimates, corrections and missing events.

How do predictions work?

Choose a route and the same departure time in Insights. Comparable personal history is preferred when enough samples exist. Prediction sources and sample counts are displayed. Early map estimates are approximate; forecasts do not guarantee arrival.

How do I sync, back up or remove my data?

In Settings, optional iCloud Drive two-way sync uses your device's iCloud account. It is off by default and resumes after recording. Both devices need the same account, iCloud Drive and available storage. Open the app and check sync status; iOS controls transfer timing. Manual full backup and restore are also available. The app database is excluded from system device backups.

Delete individual trips in History or use the clearing controls in Settings. Turning sync off leaves cloud files in place. For complete cloud removal, first disable sync on all devices, then remove the CommuteTick sync folder in Files/iCloud Drive and, where appropriate, Recently Deleted. Deletions can propagate when sync is enabled. Keep an independent backup you intend to preserve.

What is included in a share?

Full routes, endpoints and exact times are included by default. Use the preview and optional hiding controls before sharing. Add Photos permission is requested only when saving an image; full library reading is not required.

How do I report a problem?

Email octmon@qq.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, steps and any error message. Screenshots are optional. Please remove private locations and do not attach a complete backup unless you deliberately want to disclose it. Never send passwords or Apple Account credentials.

常见问题

如何开始记录？

在「今天」选择自由记录或常用路线，点击「开始记录」，允许使用期间定位并开启精确位置。可以在实时地图查看当前位置与轨迹，也可以在显示系统定位指示时切换到后台继续记录。记录页和全屏地图均可暂停、继续和结束。

可以导航或识别乘坐公交地铁吗？

不提供导航，请同时使用常用导航软件。公交站、地铁站作为自驾途经的时间节点，经过时间不代表乘坐或进入车站。

为什么有缺失或估算时刻？

定位信号、隧道、精度、暂停、权限变化及强制关闭可能造成缺口。应用会区分实测、估算、修正与缺失，不补造实际经过时间。

如何查看预测？

在「统计与预测」选择路线和固定出发时间。有足够可比样本时优先使用个人历史，并展示来源和样本数；初始地图粗估仅供参考，不保证到达时刻。

如何同步、备份和删除？

设置中的 iCloud 云盘双向同步默认关闭，开启后使用系统 iCloud 账号，记录结束后恢复同步。设备需要同一账号、已开启云盘且有可用空间。打开应用检查同步状态，上传下载时机由 iOS 决定。也可手动导出和恢复完整备份；应用数据库不进入系统设备备份。

在历史中删除单次行程，或使用设置中的清理选项。关闭同步保留已有云文件。删除全部云副本时，先在所有设备关闭同步，再通过「文件」或 iCloud 云盘删除 CommuteTick 同步文件夹，按需清理「最近删除」。同步开启时删除可能传播，请另存需要保留的独立备份。

分享会展示哪些信息？

默认包含完整路线、起终点和具体时刻，请检查预览并使用隐藏开关。仅在保存图片时申请添加照片权限，不需要读取整个相册。

如何反馈问题？

请发邮件至 octmon@qq.com，说明 iPhone 型号、iOS 及应用版本、操作步骤和错误信息。截图可选，请先遮去私人位置，除非明确愿意披露，不要发送完整备份，也不要发送密码或 Apple 账户凭据。
