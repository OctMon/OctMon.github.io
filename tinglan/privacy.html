Privacy Policy

This policy describes Tinglan: Five Tones (听岚五音), provided by the Tinglan: Five Tones developer. Contact: octmon@qq.com. The app is free, requires no account, and has no advertising, tracking, analytics or third-party SDKs. The app itself does not collect or upload personal data. Voluntary support email and public website access are described below.

1. Offline listening and information on your device

Music, five-tone introductions, research summaries and these documents are bundled with the app. Choosing a tone or playing a recording does not send a request to a developer server. The current tone selection is held in memory. The most recent recording, playback position and playback status are stored locally in a container shared only by this app and its widgets, so the widgets can show your listening and you can continue later. Reopening the app does not automatically start audio. The app does not maintain a listening-history list or provide cloud synchronization.

Tinglan: Five Tones does not read your health records or use HealthKit. It does not request microphone, location, photo-library, contacts or advertising-tracking access.

2. System audio and diagnostics

Apple audio frameworks handle local playback, background audio and lock-screen controls. The current recording title, performer, artwork and playback progress are provided to system media controls. This is not an upload to the developer. Apple may process system diagnostics according to your device settings and its privacy policy.

3. Reference links and this website

Reading the bundled documents does not require the internet. If you choose an external research, license or website link, the app opens the system browser. The destination receives the network information needed to serve the request and applies its own privacy policy.

The public pages at www.octmon.com/tinglan are hosted through GitHub Pages. Accessing them can expose your IP address, requested page, request time and browser information to the hosting provider under its privacy terms. These Tinglan: Five Tones pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, tracking pixels, embedded third-party content or external fonts. We do not add cookies or use browser storage on these pages.

4. Support correspondence

If you email octmon@qq.com, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. This information is used to answer your request and investigate the reported issue, and is retained only as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. Email providers process delivery under their own terms.

Do not include health records or other sensitive information unless you intentionally choose to disclose it. The app never sends an email or attachment automatically.

5. Your choices and contact

You can use listening and offline reading without an account or network connection. You choose whether to open external links or send support email. Uninstalling removes the installed app and its bundled files; it does not remove correspondence you sent to support or data independently held by external websites.

For questions about support correspondence, privacy requests or rights available under applicable law, contact octmon@qq.com. There is no developer-held listening-history database to retrieve or delete.

6. Children and policy changes

Tinglan: Five Tones is a general music-listening and educational app, not a product directed specifically to children. It has no child advertising or app-account registration. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the support mailbox, contact us.

We may revise this policy when functionality changes. The effective date identifies this version. Updated web pages may become available before an installed app receives its next update; the installed copy describes that app version.
