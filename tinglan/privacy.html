Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Tinglan: Five Tones handles information. Contact: octmondev@gmail.com. The app requires no account and does not collect or upload personal data. It has no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.

1. Local playback data

The most recent track and playback position are stored only on your device to resume listening and are not uploaded to the developer.

2. Permissions

Tinglan does not read health records or request microphone, location, photo library, contacts or advertising tracking permissions.

3. Support emails

If you email octmondev@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. They are used only to respond to your request and resolve the issue, and kept only as needed for support and applicable obligations. The app does not automatically send emails or attachments. For questions about this information or to request its deletion, contact the same address.

4. Policy updates

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date identifies the current version.
