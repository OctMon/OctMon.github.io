Five tones. A moment of stillness.

Tinglan: Five Tones is a music companion for emotional well-being and everyday self-care. Explore traditional five-tone approaches, set aside time to unwind, and find a quieter moment through music listening, with referenced research and its limitations.

Made for quiet listening

A native iPhone app for iOS 17 and later. English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Free, no ads and no account. Included recordings, educational summaries and legal documents are available offline.

Tradition, with sources

Read about traditional tone-organ associations and clinical research in their proper context. Recording credits and open licenses remain accessible. Music availability depends on the app version.

为安静聆听而作
